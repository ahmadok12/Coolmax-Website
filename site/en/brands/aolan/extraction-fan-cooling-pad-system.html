Aolan Extraction Fan & Cooling Pad System

Exhaust fans and evaporative cooling pads (AFF series)

Negative-pressure cooling for greenhouses, poultry houses and workshops: extraction fans pull air through wet cooling pads, replacing the air in about 30 seconds. Aolan rates the system at roughly one-third of the investment and one-tenth of the electricity of air conditioning. Fans run from 3,150 to 45,800 m³/h with stainless-steel impellers.
