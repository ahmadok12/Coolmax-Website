About Aolan

Aolan (Fujian) Industry Co., Ltd researches, develops, manufactures and services evaporative air coolers, indirect evaporative (dew point) cooling systems and liquid desiccant air conditioning. Aolan brought energy-saving coolers to the Chinese market in 1998 and has since grown into a leading evaporative cooling manufacturer.

The factory runs under ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and OHSAS 18001, holds more than 40 national patents, and its products carry CE, SASO and BV approvals. Aolan coolers are exported to more than 70 countries and regions across Europe, the Americas, Australia, the Middle East, Asia and Africa.

Accessories and group control

Up to 40 industrial units can run from one controller through hubs. Diffusers, grilles, UV lamps, ozone generators, sensors and Modbus boards are available.
