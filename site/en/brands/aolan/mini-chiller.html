Aolan's new Mini Chiller, launched at the 2026 Canton Fair, combines compressor refrigeration with evaporative cooling. The indoor unit cools the air like an air conditioner, and the heat is carried by circulating water to an outdoor unit that rejects it through wet cooling pads. The result is real AC-level cooling with a COP of 5 and 30–50% lower energy use than traditional air conditioning, installed with nothing more than PVC water pipes.

Key features

  • High efficiency: saves 30–50% energy compared with traditional air conditioning (COP 5 vs about 3 for air-cooled AC)
  • Smart control: manual, remote and mobile-app control with on/off, cool/vent modes and fault alarms
  • Easy to install: indoor and outdoor units connect with PVC water pipes only, no copper or steel piping
  • Long service life and low maintenance, with an automatic self-cleaning system
  • Big airflow and long air throw for large workshops and warehouses

Applications: Industrial and commercial spaces: factory workshops, warehousing and logistics, pharmaceutical manufacturing, offices and showrooms.

How it works

The indoor unit works like an air conditioner: a compressor, evaporator and expansion valve cool the room air. Instead of a hot air-cooled condenser, the heat goes into circulating water, which a pump sends to the outdoor unit. There, outdoor air is drawn through wet cooling pads and the heat leaves by evaporation.

Mini Chiller vs floor-standing AC

One Mini Chiller replaces two to five typical floor-standing units and uses 30–50% less electricity for the same cooling.
