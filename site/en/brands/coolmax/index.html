About Coolmax

Coolmax is the evaporative cooler range we sell every day from our store in Pakistan. It covers the full job list of a cooling dealer: compact window units, mobile coolers with a ducted top outlet, and roof or wall-mounted industrial machines up to 50,000 m³/h.

Choose centrifugal models for quieter running and higher static pressure on duct runs, or axial models for the most air per watt. Most series come with top, side or down discharge, a standard or large water tank, and single-speed or inverter control, so one container can serve homes, shops, workshops and factories.

T = top discharge, S = side, D = down discharge. -L = large water tank.
