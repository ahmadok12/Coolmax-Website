About PureBreeze

PureBreeze is our new home cooler brand for 2026. Its hero product is the BLDC cube cooler shown for the first time at this year's Canton Fair: a centrifugal cooler with a brushless DC motor that draws 450–1,100 W, a booster air vent, 120° wide-angle air supply and a high-density cooling pad.

Around it sits a complete retail range: tall duct-top coolers up to 11,000 m³/h, a 28,000 m³/h big-room cooler, slim towers, glass-top room coolers and compact bedroom units. Every model runs on 110–240 V and is quoted with 40HQ loading quantities.

R / C = remote control, A = mechanical control, G = glass top cover.
