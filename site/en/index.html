Why evaporative

Why evaporative cooling sells in hot, dry markets

Four reasons dealers across Pakistan and the Gulf keep reordering evaporative coolers every summer.

Low running cost

A 18,000 m³/h industrial unit draws about 1.1 kW. That is roughly a tenth of the electricity of compressor AC for the same space.

Fresh air, not recirculated

Evaporative coolers push 100% fresh, filtered air and work with doors and shutters open, ideal for factories, warehouses and markets.

Stronger when it is hotter

Efficiency rises as the air gets hotter and drier. At 45 °C and 20% humidity, supply air leaves the cooler at about 29 °C.

Low upfront investment

Equipment cost is a fraction of a refrigerated system, with no compressor, no refrigerant and simple maintenance.

Questions

Wholesale FAQ

Do you sell wholesale only?

Wholesale, dealer and project orders are our focus. Retail customers are welcome at our store in Pakistan.

Do you supply Saudi Arabia, the UAE and other Gulf countries?

Yes. Send your city or destination port and quantity, and we will quote with container loading and shipping options.

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the model and on whether goods ship from our Pakistan stock or directly from the factory. Send your requirement and we will confirm the MOQ.

How many coolers fit in a 40-foot container?

Each product page lists the factory's 40HQ loading quantity. For example, the Aolan Wind Supremo loads 40 units assembled or up to 70 units SKD.

Are the coolers SASO certified for Saudi Arabia?

Certificates differ by brand and model. Aolan states CE, SASO and BV approvals. Ask us for the certificate for your exact model before ordering.

Which voltage do the coolers use?

Home and portable models run on 220–240 V single-phase, and most list 110/220 V, 50/60 Hz. Large industrial units are 380–415 V 3-phase, with single-phase versions up to 18,000 m³/h.

Do evaporative coolers work in humid areas?

They work best in hot, dry air. In humid coastal areas they still provide strong fresh-air ventilation, and we will recommend the right model and air-change rate.
